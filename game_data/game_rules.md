# How to Play

## Starting the Game
### Build your character chosing:

- **An Action Hero Name**
- **A Catchphase**
- **Three Abilities**

## Character Skills

### You have 9 key skills.

- **🏃 Stunts:** Parkour, climbing, acrobatics, speed
- **👊 Brawl:** Hand-to-hand combat, physical confrontation
- **💪 Tough:** Endurance, resistance, survival, willpower
- **💻 Tech:** Computer systems, digital security, technology
- **🔫 Weapons:** Firearms, melee weapons, ranged combat
- **🚗 Drive:** Vehicle operation, chases, piloting
- **🥷 Sneak:** Stealth, infiltration, subterfuge
- **🧠 Wits** Investigation, knowledge, problem-solving
- **😎 Hot:** Social interaction, charm, persuasion, charisma

### Each skill starts at a d4.

## Blowing Up

Every time an action hero gets the highest value on their die, it **"blows up"** and that skill moves up to the next die type.

Roll that new die and add the results to the current check.

If that new roll is also the highest value, the skill continues to **blow up.**

Repeat this process until you reach a d20.

### Example:
- You roll a d6 for a Weapons check
- You roll a 6 → The 6 counts, roll again!
- You roll another 6 → Now you have 12, roll again!
- You roll a 3 → Final result is 15 (6+6+3)

This explosive mechanic creates unpredictable, escalating action that defines the "Never Stop Blowing Up" experience!

## Prepared Actions

When an Action Hero has time to prepare for a skill check and is not under stress, they  an use a **prepared action**. Instead of a rolling a die, they take half the die value for  that stat.

## Turbo Tokens

When an Action Hero fails a check they get a **Turbo Token**, which can be spent one-to-one to increase the value of a die roll.

If a die roll is increased to its highest value, it triggers a blow up as if the highest value had been rolled. Spent **Turbo Tokens** are placed on a player-specific tray in front of the board.

If both characters are in the same scene, players can spend **Turbo Tokens** for friends, but at 2x cost.

### Example:
- You have 3 **Turbo Tokens**
- You roll a d6 for a Weapons check
- You roll a 5 → You add 1 **Turbo Token** to the roll, making it a 6 and trigger it to **Blow Up**.
- You now roll a d8 → Getting a 6.  You add your remaining 2 **Turbo Tokens** to the roll, blowing it up again!
- You now roll a d10, getting a 3 → Final result is 17 (6+8+3).

## Injury

There are three injury levels.
- **Superficial**
- **Severe**
- **Adrenalized**

When an Action Hero fails a **Tough Check** by 5 or more, they take an **Injury Level**.

At **Severe Injury**, players must spend double the amount of **Turbo Tokens** (i.e., 2 Turbo Tokens to Blow Up a 5 on a d6).

At the **Adrenalized** injury level, the injured character receives 10 **Turbo Tokens**.

When an Adrenalized character fails a **Tough Check**, they become either **Incapacitated** or **Dead**, depending on the attacker’s intention.

## Quick Play Tips

1. **Start Small:** Begin with d4 or d6 dice and work your way up
2. **Embrace Explosions:** When dice explode, lean into the chaos and escalation
3. **Use Turbo Tokens Wisely:** Save them for crucial moments or dramatic reveals
4. **Work as a Team:** Group abilities are powerful when coordinated
5. **Keep it Moving:** The game thrives on momentum and escalating action

## Difficulty Classes

- **Easy:** DC 5-8
- **Moderate:** DC 9-12
- **Hard:** DC 13-16
- **Very Hard:** DC 17-20
- **Nearly Impossible:** DC 21+

# Abilities

At the end of each session, players may use remaining **Turbo Tokens** to purchase extra abilities. Individual abilities cost 2 Turbo Tokens.

- **👮 By the Book:** Lower the DC of a Hot Check by 3 when interacting with authority figures
- **🔓 Burglar:** Lower the DC of a Sneak Check by 3 when entering a protected location
- **🤝 Connected:** Lower the DC of a Hot Check by 3 when searching for a helpful ally
- **💣 Demolitions:** Lower the DC of a Tech Check by 3 when explosives are involved
- **⚔️ Duelist:** Lower the DC of a Weapons Check by 3 against someone wielding the same weapon
- **🔗 Escape Artist:** Lower the DC of a Sneak Check by 3 when escaping restraints or imprisonment
- **✨ Flashy:** Lower the DC of a Hot Check by 5 if it's the first roll after you've Blown Up
- **🛡️ Grit:** Lower the DC of a Tough Check by 3 when resisting a Brawl Check
- **💻 Hacker:** Lower the DC of a Tech Check by 3 when breaking into a computer database
- **🔧 Hotwire:** When interacting with vehicles, use Drive instead of Tech
- **⭐ Inspiring:** Each time you succeed at a check, an ally of your choice receives 1 Turbo Token
- **🔍 Interrogator:** Lower the DC of a Wits Check by 3 to draw information out of an opponent
- **🦘 Leap of Faith:** Lower the DC of a Stunts Check by 3 when making a jump that could injure you
- **🤗 Loyal:** Your Turbo Tokens give friends +1 at a one-to-one rate
- **🍀 Lucky:** Spend 2 Turbo Tokens to reroll any check *(Once Per Episode)*
- **🥋 Martial Artist:** Spend a Turbo Token to force an enemy to resist your Brawl with Wits instead of Tough
- **😠 Menacing:** Use Brawl instead of Hot when intimidating an NPC
- **🧘 Nerves of Steel:** Spend a Turbo Token to treat a snap decision as a Prepared Action
- **🎭 Poker Face:** When attempting to conceal the truth, use Tough instead of Hot
- **🎒 Prepared:** Spend 2 Turbo Tokens to just happen to have one commonplace item with you *(GM's Discretion)*
- **🛡️ Protector:** Lower the DC of a check by 3 when defending your friends
- **🩹 Quick Healing:** Recover one injury level at the end of an encounter
- **💪 Relentless:** Gain 2 Turbo Tokens when you fail a check
- **🛡️ Resilient:** Turbo Tokens are worth double when used to boost against an attack that targets you
- **⚡ Skilled:** Increase your die type for one skill by 1 for the purposes of halving
- **😎 Smokin':** Lower the DC of a Hot Check by 3 when making a first impression
- **👤 Stealthy:** Lower the DC of a Sneak Check by 3 when trying to avoid being seen
- **🎯 Trained:** -1 to DCs for a Stat of your choice
- **📚 Studied:** *(Replaces Trained)* -3 to DCs for Stat of your choice
- **🎓 Mastery:** *(Replaces Studied)* -5 to DCs for a Stat of your choice
- **🚗 Transporter:** Lower the DC of a Drive Check by 3 to avoid pursuers
- **🔫 Trouble Maker:** Spend a Turbo Token to locate and receive help from a criminal network *(GM's Discretion)*
- **💰 Wealthy:** Spend a Turbo Token to ease a bad situation with cash
- **🃏 Wild Card:** You are a Wild Card

# Group Suites

New ‘group suites’ of abilities are unlocked for purchase once all players reach a higher die type (i.e., one d6 from every player). Abilities from group suites cost 10 turbo tokens for an individual, or 18 for the entire group.

- **🕵️ Criminal Conspiracy (d6):** Heists, cons, and criminal operations
  - **Hot:** Roll Hot in response to the first attack of an encounter to dissuade an opponent.
  - **Item:** When in a new location, produce a single useful item.
  - **Tech:** Once per episode, add your Tech Die to a Sneak Die.
- **👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 La Familia (d6):** Family bonds and mutual support
  - **Skill Die:** Lend a teammate a skill die.
  - **Tokens:** Spend tokens for other people at a 1:1 exchange rate.
  - **Tough:** Roll Tough on someone else's behalf.
- **🏍️ Diesel Circus (d8):** Vehicle-based mayhem and stunts
  - **Double Explosion:** Doubles your amount of tokens.
  - **Drive Check:** On a successful Drive Check, make another skill check.
  - **Injury Advantage:** Roll twice on the first roll after an injury.
- **🎖️ Tactical Command (d8):** Military precision and coordination
  - **Reroll:** Reroll on a failure.
  - **Shared Tokens:** Spend Turbo Tokens across scenes.
  - **Token Gain:** Characters gain a Turbo Token at the end of a scene where they have zero.
- **🎯 Alpha Squad (d10):** Elite team operations
  - **Group Explosion:** In a scene where everyone uses a different skill, reduce the range to Blow Up by 1.
  - **Skill Add:** Two people roll the same skill and add the totals together.
  - **Suit Up:** The group may 'suit up' and each take 2 Turbo Tokens.
- **💀 Marauders (d10):** Destructive force and chaos
  - **+10:** Beating an opponent by more than 10 means you defeat an additional opponent.
  - **Destroyer:** Gain a Turbo Token on any turn where you destroy an object.
  - **Firestarter:** Start a fire, short circuit electronics, or dissolve a structure as part of any action.
- **🎩 The Continentals (d12):** Sophisticated espionage
  - **Hot Checks:** Two successful Hot Checks auto-succeeds on stealing from an opponent.
  - **Melee:** Lower the DC of a Weapons Checks using melee weapons.
  - **Wits:** Turbo Tokens for Wits help increase the die type.
- **🏆 The Ones (d12):** Peak performance and excellence
  - **Max Roll:** Treat a Nat 1 as a max die roll.
  - **Reroll:** Reroll a failure with a different skill.
  - **Turbo Tokens:** Accept a Nat 1 to gain half the Turbo Tokens of the die value.
- **👻 Bustin' Makes Me Feel Good (d20):** Supernatural encounters
  - **GM:** Become the GM for 60 seconds.
  - **Group Explosion:** Everybody else at the table graduates all of their lowest die type up by one.
  - **Track Restart:** Restart that skill track, now rolling a second die and taking the better result.
