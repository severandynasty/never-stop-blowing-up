- **An Action Hero Name**

## Character Stats

### The Six Stats

- **🔫 Weapons:** Firearms, melee weapons, ranged combat
- **👊 Brawl:** Hand-to-hand combat, physical confrontation
- **😎 Hot:** Social interaction, charm, persuasion, charisma
- **🚗 Drive:** Vehicle operation, chases, piloting
- **💪 Tough:** Endurance, resistance, survival, willpower
- **🥷 Sneak:** Stealth, infiltration, subterfuge

## Turbo Tokens

### How to Spend Turbo Tokens:
- Add an extra die to any roll
- Activate special player abilities
- Boost damage or effects
- Influence the narrative in dramatic ways
- Power group abilities

### How to Gain Turbo Tokens:
- Rolling maximum value on any die
- Achieving dramatic story moments
- Group accomplishments and teamwork
- GM awards for exceptional roleplay

## Group Suites

Your group chooses a suite that defines your team's specialty and unlocks unique group abilities:

- **🕵️ Criminal Conspiracy (d6):** Heists, cons, and criminal operations
- **👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 La Familia (d6):** Family bonds and mutual support
- **🏍️ Diesel Circus (d8):** Vehicle-based mayhem and stunts
- **🎖️ Tactical Command (d8):** Military precision and coordination
- **🎯 Alpha Squad (d10):** Elite team operations
- **💀 Marauders (d10):** Destructive force and chaos
- **🎩 The Continentals (d12):** Sophisticated espionage
- **🏆 The Ones (d12):** Peak performance and excellence
- **👻 Bustin' Makes Me Feel Good (d20):** Supernatural encounters

## Quick Play Tips

1. **Start Small:** Begin with d4 or d6 dice and work your way up
2. **Embrace Explosions:** When dice explode, lean into the chaos and escalation
3. **Use Turbo Tokens Wisely:** Save them for crucial moments or dramatic reveals
4. **Work as a Team:** Group abilities are powerful when coordinated
5. **Keep it Moving:** The game thrives on momentum and escalating action

# Quick Reference Guide

## 🎲 Dice Rolling

- **Basic Roll:** Roll your stat die + any applicable modifiers
- **Exploding Dice:** Max result = roll again and add
- **Success:** Meet or exceed the Difficulty Class (DC)
- **Failure:** Take consequences, gain narrative control

## 🎯 Difficulty Classes

- **Easy:** DC 5-8
- **Moderate:** DC 9-12
- **Hard:** DC 13-16
- **Very Hard:** DC 17-20
- **Nearly Impossible:** DC 21+

## ⚡ Turbo Token Uses

- **Extra Die:** Add +1d6 to any roll
- **Boost Damage:** +3 damage to attacks
- **Narrative Control:** Add details to the scene
- **Special Abilities:** Activate player/group abilities
- **Avoid Consequences:** Reduce injury or setback

## 🏥 Injuries

- **1 Injury:** -1 to all rolls
- **2 Injuries:** -2 to all rolls
- **3 Injuries:** Incapacitated, out of action
